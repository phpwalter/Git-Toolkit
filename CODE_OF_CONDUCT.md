# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participation in Git Toolkit should be professional, technically focused, and respectful. Contributors are expected to discuss ideas, implementations, defects, and tradeoffs without personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or abuse.

Constructive disagreement is welcome. Review comments should address observable behavior, requirements, tests, security, maintainability, compatibility, or documentation rather than the individual proposing a change.

## Expected behavior

Examples of constructive participation include:

- giving specific, actionable technical feedback;
- acknowledging uncertainty and correcting mistakes;
- keeping issue and review discussions relevant to the project;
- respecting security-reporting boundaries and private information;
- avoiding disclosure of credentials, private repository data, or personal information;
- helping maintain deterministic, testable project behavior.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption, doxxing, publication of private information without permission, or repeated bad-faith behavior after maintainer direction.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove abusive or disruptive content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its contributors. Enforcement decisions should be proportional to the conduct and documented when practical.

Security reports containing sensitive details should be handled through private reporting channels rather than public issues.
